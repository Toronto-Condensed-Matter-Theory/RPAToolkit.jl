MeanFieldToolkit interface

These take a converged MeanFieldToolkit TBMFTModel. See Collective modes and mean-field theory.

RPAToolkit.MeanFieldInterface.rpa_model — Function
rpa_model(mft; residual = nothing, tol = 1e-6) -> NamedTuple

The Hartree-Fock bands of a converged MeanFieldToolkit TBMFTModel, ready for dynamic_bubble / stored_bubble: (bands, mu, beta, filling, unitcell), with bands the BandModel of mft.model.uc (the free hoppings plus the decoupled interaction) and mu, beta its chemical potential and inverse temperature.

Pass the self-consistency residual (maximum(abs, sc.VOuts[end] - sc.VIns[end])) to have it checked against tol: MeanFieldToolkit builds the final unit cell from the last INPUT order parameters, so the bands, and with them the Goldstone mode of a broken continuous symmetry, are off by O(residual).

RPAToolkit.MeanFieldInterface.rpa_vertex — Function
rpa_vertex(mft; interactions = nothing) -> Function

q -> V(q): the TDHF vertex of a MeanFieldToolkit TBMFTModel's interactions, in the layout of interaction (4 × n_sites square, channel innermost), so that dynamic_rpa(dynamic_bubble(...), V.(qs)) is the linear response of the Hartree-Fock state.

On-site interactions (IntraQuarticToHopping) enter with their full Hartree and Fock kernel (quartic_onsite_vertex): the same interaction tensor MeanFieldToolkit decoupled, so a spontaneously broken continuous symmetry gives an exact Goldstone mode. Inter-site interactions (InterQuarticToHopping) enter with their Hartree part (quartic_hartree_vertex); their exchange part renormalizes the bands but is not in the vertex, and a warning says so. interactions (indices into mft.Interactions) keeps only those terms, e.g. to take the on-site vertex from here and a long-range tail from coulomb_2d, summing the matrices.
