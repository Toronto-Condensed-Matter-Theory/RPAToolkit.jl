RPAToolkit.Interactions

RPAToolkit.Interactions.DENSITY_CHANNELS — Constant
DENSITY_CHANNELS

A density-density term V n_a n_b in the (N, X, Y, Z) channel slots: diag(-1, 0, 0, 0), i.e. charge only (slot N holds -U^(c), see HUBBARD_CHANNELS). A repulsive V > 0 screens the charge channel and never enhances the spin channels.

Register one direction per bond: interaction adds the Hermitian conjugate itself.

RPAToolkit.Interactions.HUBBARD_CHANNELS — Constant
HUBBARD_CHANNELS

The on-site Hubbard term U n_up n_dn in the (N, X, Y, Z) channel slots: diag(-1, +1, +1, +1).

These are the physical channels (VERTEX_SLOTS). With chi_RPA = (1 - chi V)^-1 chi and a positive bare chi, a positive entry is enhanced: slots X, Y and Z carry the Stoner (spin) vertex U^(s) and slot N carries the charge vertex negated, -U^(c) – the layout channel_blocks uses. Written in the Pauli channels, the antisymmetrized Hubbard vertex is exactly this matrix, so the layout stays right when spin-orbit coupling makes chi mix the channels.

interaction adds every registered bond AND its Hermitian conjugate, on-site bonds included, so an on-site bond lands in its own block twice: register U/2 .* HUBBARD_CHANNELS to get U.

RPAToolkit.Interactions.INTERACTION_CONVENTION — Constant
INTERACTION_CONVENTION

The Fourier convention of every interaction matrix: a bond (i, j, offset) with matrix M enters $V_{ij}(q)$ as $M e^{+iq\cdot\delta}$, $\delta$ the lattice vector of offset, which is the Bloch convention of TightBindingToolkit's $H(k)$ for the same bond. It is the one for which $\chi_\mathrm{RPA} = (1 - \chi V)^{-1}\chi$ is the RPA: combine_chis's $\chi_{ij}(q)$ is the response of the $e^{iq\cdot R}$ component of $O_i$ to a field $e^{iq\cdot R}$ on $O_j$, and the mean field of a pair interaction acting on that component is $\sum_R e^{+iq\cdot R} v_{ij}(R)$. Checked against an exact real-space Hartree RPA on a periodic cluster (test/testrparealspace.jl).

Until 2026-11-12 interaction used $e^{-iq\cdot\delta}$, i.e. $V(-q) = V(q)^T$. Identical for on-site interactions and for any lattice where every bond comes with its reverse (one site per cell), but on a multi-site cell a non-local interaction entered transposed: the honeycomb example's $1/r$ charge response was off by 30-40%. run_rpa records this string as "interaction_convention".

RPAToolkit.Interactions.hubbard_coulomb_interactions — Method
hubbard_coulomb_interactions(uc; onsite, range) -> (params, cases)

On-site Hubbard plus a bare 1/r density-density tail, for a 2D unit cell with one orbital per basis site, in the form run_rpa reads from an interactions file:

  • params: a Vector{Param}. The first holds the on-site Hubbard bonds, registered at onsite / 2 (see HUBBARD_CHANNELS). The rest hold one (1/r) .* DENSITY_CHANNELS bond each, one direction per pair of sites with 0 < r <= range.
  • cases: "Hubbard" (the on-site term alone) and "Coulomb" (on-site plus the tail), each a vector of Param weights in the order of params.

Lengths are in the units of uc.primitives, and onsite in units of the tail's 1/r. The RPA strength then scales the whole interaction. At momentum q the assembled matrix has -U^(c)(q) = -(onsite + V(q)) in slot N and U^(s) = onsite in slots X, Y and Z on every site, with V(q) = sum_{0 < |δ| <= range} e^{+i q.δ} / |δ|.

Save the returned values with the interaction unit cell and the slot layout under the keys "parameters", "values", "unit cell" and "vertex_slots" => VERTEX_SLOTS; run_rpa checks that unit cell against the model's and refuses a file without the layout.

RPAToolkit.Interactions.interaction — Method
interaction(strength, k; primitives, subs, localDim, lookup) -> Matrix{ComplexF64}
interaction(strength, k, params::Vector{<:Param}; primitives, subs, localDim)
interaction(strength, k, unitcell::UnitCell)

The interaction matrix V(k) of size subs * localDim, scaled by strength.

Each entry (i, j, offset) => M of the bond lookup (a TightBindingToolkit.Lookup) adds M e^{+i k.δ} to block (i, j) and its Hermitian conjugate M' e^{-i k.δ} to block (j, i), with δ = sum(offset .* primitives): the Bloch convention of TightBindingToolkit's H(k) for the same bond (INTERACTION_CONVENTION). On-site bonds are no exception, so they count twice. The channel index (localDim, 4 for N, X, Y, Z) is innermost.

k may also be a vector of momenta, in which case a vector of matrices is returned. interaction(s, k) == s * interaction(1.0, k) exactly.

RPAToolkit.Interactions.quartic_hartree_vertex — Method
quartic_hartree_vertex(U) -> (V_ij, V_ji)

The Hartree part of the TDHF vertex of a two-site interaction $\sum_{abcd} U_{abcd}\, c^\dagger_{ia} c_{ib} c^\dagger_{jc} c_{jd}$: the field on site i from the density on site j, and on j from i (MeanFieldToolkit's InterQuarticToHopping on-site terms). Its exchange part renormalizes the bond hopping instead, which has no representation on per-site channels.

source
RPAToolkit.Interactions.quartic_onsite_vertex — Method
quartic_onsite_vertex(U) -> Matrix{ComplexF64}

The TDHF vertex of an on-site interaction $\sum_{abcd} U_{abcd}\, c^\dagger_a c_b c^\dagger_c c_d$ (one spin-1/2 site, MeanFieldToolkit's convention): Hartree and Fock together, $K_{abcd} = U_{abcd} + U_{cdab} - U_{cbad} - U_{adcb}$, exactly the kernel of MeanFieldToolkit's IntraQuarticToHopping. See tdhf_channel_vertex.

RPAToolkit.Interactions.tdhf_channel_vertex — Method
tdhf_channel_vertex(K) -> Matrix{ComplexF64}

The 4×4 block, in the (N, X, Y, Z) slots of interaction, of a mean-field kernel K[a, b, c, d] on spin-1/2 sites: the linear change of the mean-field hopping $\delta t_{ab} = \sum_{cd} K_{abcd}\,\delta\langle c^\dagger_c c_d\rangle$ (a, b on the site the field acts on, c, d on the site whose density changed). That is the time-dependent Hartree-Fock kernel $\delta\Sigma/\delta\rho$, so the RPA built from it is the linear response of the same mean-field theory.

With channel operators $O_p = c^\dagger M_p c$, $M_p = (\sigma^p)^T/2$ (transposed, the convention the stored bubble carries; see stored_bubble), the kernel acts as $\varphi_p = \sum_q W_{pq}\,\delta\langle O_q\rangle$ with $W_{pq} = \sum \overline{M_p}_{ab} K_{abcd} \overline{M_q}_{cd} / \mathrm{Tr}(M^2)^2$, and the slot matrix is $-W/2$: minus, because chi is a positive response, and one half, because chi is the per-spin bubble (twice the susceptibility of the sigma/2 operators). For $U n_\uparrow n_\downarrow$ this is exactly HUBBARD_CHANNELS.* U.
