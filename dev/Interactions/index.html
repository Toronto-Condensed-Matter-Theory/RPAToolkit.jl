RPAToolkit.Interactions

RPAToolkit.Interactions.DENSITY_CHANNELS — Constant
DENSITY_CHANNELS

A density-density term V n_a n_b in the (N, X, Y, Z) channel slots: diag(0, 0, 0, -1), i.e. charge only (slot 4 holds -U^(c), see HUBBARD_CHANNELS). A repulsive V > 0 screens the charge channel and never enhances the spin channel.

Register one direction per bond: interaction adds the Hermitian conjugate itself.

RPAToolkit.Interactions.HUBBARD_CHANNELS — Constant
HUBBARD_CHANNELS

The on-site Hubbard term U n_up n_dn in the (N, X, Y, Z) channel slots: diag(+1, 0, 0, -1).

With chi_RPA = (1 - chi V)^-1 chi and a positive bare chi, a positive entry is enhanced. Slot 1 therefore carries the Stoner (spin) vertex U^(s) and slot 4 carries the charge vertex negated, -U^(c) – the layout channel_blocks uses.

interaction adds every registered bond AND its Hermitian conjugate, on-site bonds included, so an on-site bond lands in its own block twice: register U/2 .* HUBBARD_CHANNELS to get U.

RPAToolkit.Interactions.hubbard_coulomb_interactions — Method
hubbard_coulomb_interactions(uc; onsite, range) -> (params, cases)

On-site Hubbard plus a bare 1/r density-density tail, for a 2D unit cell with one orbital per basis site, in the form run_rpa reads from an interactions file:

  • params: a Vector{Param}. The first holds the on-site Hubbard bonds, registered at onsite / 2 (see HUBBARD_CHANNELS). The rest hold one (1/r) .* DENSITY_CHANNELS bond each, one direction per pair of sites with 0 < r <= range.
  • cases: "Hubbard" (the on-site term alone) and "Coulomb" (on-site plus the tail), each a vector of Param weights in the order of params.

Lengths are in the units of uc.primitives, and onsite in units of the tail's 1/r. The RPA strength then scales the whole interaction. At momentum q the assembled matrix has U^(s) = onsite in slot 1 and -U^(c)(q) = -(onsite + V(q)) in slot 4 on every site, with V(q) = sum_{0 < |δ| <= range} e^{-i q.δ} / |δ|.

Save the returned values with the interaction unit cell under the keys "parameters", "values" and "unit cell"; run_rpa checks that unit cell against the model's.

RPAToolkit.Interactions.interaction — Method
interaction(strength, k; primitives, subs, localDim, lookup) -> Matrix{ComplexF64}
interaction(strength, k, params::Vector{<:Param}; primitives, subs, localDim)
interaction(strength, k, unitcell::UnitCell)

The interaction matrix V(k) of size subs * localDim, scaled by strength.

Each entry (i, j, offset) => M of the bond lookup (a TightBindingToolkit.Lookup) adds M e^{-i k.δ} to block (i, j) and its Hermitian conjugate M' e^{+i k.δ} to block (j, i), with δ = sum(offset .* primitives). On-site bonds are no exception, so they count twice. The channel index (localDim, 4 for N, X, Y, Z) is innermost.

k may also be a vector of momenta, in which case a vector of matrices is returned. interaction(s, k) == s * interaction(1.0, k) exactly.
