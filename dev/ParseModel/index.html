RPAToolkit.parse_model

RPAToolkit.parse_model.parse_unitcell — Method
parse_unitcell(uc::UnitCell) -> Dict{String, Any}
parse_unitcell(uc::UnitCell, fileName)

The hopping model of a TightBindingToolkit UnitCell in serialized form (the bare stage's BandModel and spin_symmetry are built from it): "units" (primitives, padded to 3D, as columns), "orbital_positions" (one column per spin-orbital), and "hopping matrices" / "hopping offsets" with every bond's Hermitian conjugate filled in at the opposite offset. With a fileName, writes it as an .npz.
