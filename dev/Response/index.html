RPAToolkit.Response.find_instability — Method
find_instability(chis, ks, V_unit; steps = 32, lower = 0.0, upper = 10.0, primitives)
find_instability(chis, ks; steps, lower, upper, primitives, subs, localDim, lookup)

The critical interaction strength: a bisection on [lower, upper] for the smallest s at which chi_RPA = (1 - chi s V)^-1 chi acquires a negative eigenvalue at some momentum. V_unit are the interaction matrices at unit strength on the same momenta ks as chis; the second form builds them from a bond lookup with interaction. If nothing goes unstable below upper, the result is upper (to the bisection's resolution).

Returns a Dict with "critical strength" plus, from chi_RPA at that strength, the minima and maxima entries and the momenta they sit at ("minimum momentum", "maximum momentum", and their "... reciprocal momentum" in units of the reciprocal vectors).

Only one chi_RPA is diagonalized, at the end. For a positive semidefinite chi, chi^{1/2} (1 - s A)^-1 chi^{1/2} with A = chi^{1/2} V chi^{1/2} has a negative eigenvalue exactly when s λ > 1 for an eigenvalue λ of chi V (Sylvester's law of inertia), so every bisection step compares s against the largest such λ over all momenta, computed once.

RPAToolkit.Response.perform_RPA — Method
perform_RPA(chi, V; return_matrix = false)

The RPA susceptibility chi_RPA = (1 - chi V)^-1 chi at one momentum, returned as the matrix (return_matrix = true) or as its (eigenvalues, eigenvectors), sorted by real part.

With vectors of chi and V (one per momentum), returns a vector of those.
