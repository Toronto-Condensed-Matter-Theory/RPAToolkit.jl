RPAToolkit.Dynamics

RPAToolkit.Dynamics.coulomb_2d — Method
coulomb_2d(primitives, positions; e2 = 1.0, eps = 1.0, onsite = 0.0, alpha = nothing) -> Function

The long-range Coulomb interaction $H = \frac{1}{2}\sum_{r \ne r'} \frac{e^2}{\varepsilon |r - r'|} n_r n_{r'}$ plus an on-site Hubbard onsite $\sum_r n_{r\uparrow} n_{r\downarrow}$, as q -> V(q), a 4n × 4n matrix (n = length(positions)) in interaction()'s layout, charge slot only:

V_{(i,N),(j,N)}(q) = -( onsite delta_ij + 2 v_ij(q) ),
v_ij(q) = (e^2 / eps) sum_R e^{+iq.R} / |R + b_j - b_i|      (Ewald-summed, 2 pi e^2 / (eps A |q|) as q -> 0)

The factor 2 is there because chi is the PER-SPIN bubble: the RPA then reads 1 + 2 v chi0 = 1 + v Π with Π the full density bubble, so the plasmon of a 2D electron gas comes out at $\omega_p^2 = 2\pi n e^2 q / (\varepsilon m)$. The phase convention is interaction()'s (INTERACTION_CONVENTION, $e^{+iq\cdot R}$), so the two can be added. At q = 0 the divergent G = 0 term is dropped (a neutralizing background).

RPAToolkit.Dynamics.dynamic_rpa — Method
dynamic_rpa(chi0, V) -> Matrix{Matrix{ComplexF64}}

$\chi_{RPA}(q, \omega) = (1 - \chi_0(q, \omega) V(q))^{-1} \chi_0(q, \omega)$ for chi0[iq, iw] (from dynamic_bubble) and V[iq], the interaction at each momentum in the same layout (interaction(), coulomb_2d, or one constant matrix for an on-site vertex).

RPAToolkit.Dynamics.rpa_poles — Method
rpa_poles(spectral, omegas; contrast = 5.0) -> Vector{Float64}

For each row of spectral (one momentum), the frequency of its largest peak, or NaN when that peak is less than contrast times the row's median (no sharp mode there): a quick read-out of a collective mode's dispersion. The peak is refined by a parabola through its three highest points.

RPAToolkit.Dynamics.spectral_map — Method
spectral_map(chi, qs, positions, channels) -> Matrix{Float64}

spectral_weight of chi[iq, iw] with the unfolded operators of channels at each qs[iq], as an (nq, nw) array: e.g. channels = (1, 2) for the transverse spin response of a magnet ordered along z, (0,) for the density response.

RPAToolkit.Dynamics.spectral_weight — Method
spectral_weight(chi, U) -> Float64

$\sum_c \mathrm{Im}(u_c^\dagger \chi u_c)/\pi$ over the columns of U: the spectral function of the operators U describes. Non-negative for w > 0 when chi is a retarded response.

RPAToolkit.Dynamics.unfold_vector — Method
unfold_vector(positions, q, channels) -> Matrix{ComplexF64}

One column per channel in channels (0 = N, 1 = X, 2 = Y, 3 = Z): the operator of the ORIGINAL lattice at momentum q, $O^a(q) = \sum_r e^{-iq\cdot r} c^\dagger_r \sigma^a c_r / 2$, written in the supercell's per-site channels with positions the sites of the cell,

u_{(i, a)} = e^{+i q.b_i} / sqrt(n_sites),

so that u' chi u (chi in the static pipeline's convention, channel innermost) is the original lattice's per-site susceptibility at q. Pinned numerically: a paramagnetic two-site cell, unfolded this way, reproduces the one-site bubble at every q and frequency.
