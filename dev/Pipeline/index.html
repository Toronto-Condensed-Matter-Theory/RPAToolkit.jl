RPAToolkit.Pipeline.run_bare — Method
run_bare(config) -> String

The bare-susceptibility stage.

Reads the model's "unit cell" from unitcell.julia and, for every chemical potential of the scan (mus, or fillings inverted once on the bands), computes the static bubble on the k_size × k_size grid and along the closed path through k_points. Writes one <output>_beta=<beta>_mu=<mu>.npz per mu, in the stored layout and convention (stored_bubble), and the resolved config (k-points as coordinates, the resolved mus and fillings under scan.source) to <output>_runtime_input.yml, which it returns.

Config keys: bubble (lindhard, the exact sum and the default, or dlr, the imaginary-time FFT route that scales as Nk log Nk, with w_max its spectral cutoff and dlr_err its accuracy), bubble_spin (auto, or full to skip the SU(2) factorization), bilinear_basis (per_orbital or closed, with bilinear_seed) and directions. Threaded over momenta.

RPAToolkit.Pipeline.run_rpa — Method
run_rpa(config; run_bare = false, plot = false, save_individual = false) -> Dict

The RPA stage. For every chemical potential of the bare stage and every interaction case in the interactions file, finds the critical strength with find_instability. Saves everything to rpa_output_path and returns it.

  • run_bare = true runs run_bare first.
  • plot = true saves chi_RPA along the k-path, at each of the config's strengths below the critical one, as <plots>_chi_beta=..._mu=..._interactionID=..._strength=....png.
  • save_individual = true also saves each instability on its own, as <output>_beta=..._mu=..._interactionID=<case>.jld2.

The (mu, case) pairs run in parallel over Julia threads (julia -t N); each pair is serial over momenta. Keep BLAS single-threaded (BLAS.set_num_threads(1)) so the two do not oversubscribe.

The output has one entry per mu, keyed "beta=<beta>_mu=<mu>", holding "mu", "beta", "filling", "bare_data" (the bubble file), "combined_chis" and "combined_chis_path" (from combine_chis), "chi_orientation", "spin_symmetry" (the channel pairs chi holds, see spin_sectors), "vertex_slots", "<case>_interaction" and "<case>_interaction_path" (the unit-strength V(q)), and "<case>" (the find_instability result, with the "unstable sector").

The interactions file must record "vertex_slots" => VERTEX_SLOTS. The RPA is solved one spin sector at a time: the model's spin symmetry (spin_symmetry) makes chi block diagonal in them, so a model without spin-orbit coupling solves its charge and one spin block, and one with Ising spin-orbit coupling its longitudinal (N, Z) and transverse (X, Y) blocks. On the closed bilinear basis it also holds "bilinear_basis" and "basis_dim".
